How to read this file:

- Phases hold milestones. A milestone is done when its gates pass, not when its list is ticked. `[auto]` gates are checked by CI or a script; `[you]` gates need Evan's hands or phone.
- Work happens only at the **CURRENT MILESTONE** marker. Ideas that arrive outside it go under their phase, in "Ideas, not yet in a milestone".
- Every number in Readings names its instrument, date and N. A number marked (tune) is a proposal.

**[you] gates still open.** The marker moves on while these wait for Evan's phone (moved 2026-10-24, Evan's call); none is dropped:

100. Classes are attended where you stand: there is no campus on the map, no walk to it and no classmates among the people. You can't drop a course once enrolled, and a term can't be taken part-way. The catalog is one list in one order, with no majors, placement or prerequisites.
101. Paying is simplified. Every loan is unsubsidized: a student with need would have part of it subsidized, with no interest while in school. Pell falls in a straight line between its income shares, not by the Student Aid Index, and counts the past 365 days' pay, not the year before last's. There is no lifetime limit on Pell (twelve terms) or the loans, no living costs to borrow for past the bill, and a new grace after each spell in school, where the real grace comes once. Payments behind have no consequence yet: no default at 270 days, no collections or garnished wages. Michigan's own aid (Reconnect's free tuition from 21, the Achievement Scholarship) isn't in.
102. Credentials gate hiring only. Someone already in a career keeps it whatever they finished, and a rank reached by promotion asks for nothing of its own. A preferred credential is a flat number of points (tune), not a reading: audit studies of callbacks by credential would give it. One career stands for the associate's, so pay by credential is thin there. Licences (a nurse's, a cosmetologist's, a commercial driver's) and certificates short of a degree aren't in.
